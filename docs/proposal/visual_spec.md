# Visual + Narration Spec — 5 plates (built on the abhinav-poster-style system)

Rule: each plate's NARRATION (caption, annotations, labels) adds depth the body text does not — a mechanism, a number, a consequence — never a repeat. Below each plate: the Abhinav **family to pull from your 75+** (so you can match/tweak a base image), the composition, and the exact narration lines (what to letter onto the plate).

SYSTEM (all five, one hand):
- Ground: **oxblood/maroon** dominant (`#2E151C`); P5 may use the slightly redder Batch-4 maroon `#37181E`. Cream line-work `#E8DFD0`.
- Semantics fixed: sage `#9DB886` = good/robust/accepted · amber `#D4A530` = moderate/near · warm red `#C45545` = fails/erodes/danger · terracotta `#D4835C` = the chosen/focal thing · teal `#7CBAC4` = free alternative / directional.
- One accent per plate doing every accent job. Montserrat for titles/labels (heavy condensed caps); body stays the serif.
- Honest encoding: bars from zero, axes carry unit, no 3D, no fake smoothing. Numbers are real (from the proposal/evidence).
- Caption sits with the diagram leading; keyword printed as a visible label.

## P1 — "One place, two answers"  ·  keyword: PATHWAYS
**Pull from:** Abhinav **split before/after** panel (framed diagram, vertical rule, two labelled halves) + a scene-register household on the right. Base candidates in your set: any two-panel "before/after" or the street-scene/Alexander isometric for the right half.
**Composition:** one frame, split by a thin cream rule. LEFT = a single settled 2050 block (one flat isometric mass, one date "2050", static, grey-cream). RIGHT = the SAME household drawn four times down a year axis (2026 → 2050): its car flips from cream to teal (electric), a small toll-bill bar shrinks, an acceptance line rises then dips, and the path splits into surviving (sage) and broken (red ghost) branches. Human-scale stick figure at the door on the right, true scale.
**Narration that ADDS depth (letter these on):**
- Caption (bottom): *"A model gives one 2050. A household lives a sequence. Only one of these is a plan."*
- Left label: `EQUILIBRIUM — one state, no route`
- Right annotations (each a mechanism the body only names): `fleet turns over slowly`, `acceptance carries year to year (ρ)`, `debt is due on a schedule` — the three things an equilibrium cannot hold.
- One red ghost branch tagged: `pathway breaks here — threshold crossed`.
**Leave out:** any second figure, any map. One household, one axis.

## P3 — "What memory does"  ·  keyword: ACCEPTANCE AS ADMISSIBILITY
**Pull from:** Abhinav **past/future line chart with uncertainty band** (LLM carousel) for the three agents, + the **pattern fan / convergence** motif for the instrument order. Base candidates: the "past/future" prediction chart; any "pattern fan" component-mechanics poster.
**Composition:** top two-thirds — three acceptance trajectories over 2026→2050 (three agents: a commuter, a firm, a low-income household), each a cream line; the band where acceptance drops below the threshold λ is a red zone; the line that stays above is sage. Bottom third — an **instrument fan**: the same four instruments drawn in two different orders, ending in two different fates (one sage "admissible", one red "inadmissible").
**Narration that ADDS depth:**
- Caption: *"Same instruments, different order, different fate. The order is the finding."*
- Footer equation, in plain words (not symbols): `acceptance next year = memory of last year + what you pay + what you were told`. (reduced form of eq. 2)
- Threshold label: `λ — a pathway is admissible only if acceptance stays above this every year`.
- One fan branch tagged: `charge road freight too late → window shuts`.
**Leave out:** the full latent-variable maths. One plain sentence carries it.

## P5 — "The robust front"  ·  keyword: ROBUSTNESS / REGRET / RECEIPTS
**Pull from:** Abhinav **Pareto scatter with selected point** (dotted ghost = dominated, solid = front, circled "selected" + dashed crosshairs, curved trade-off arrow). This is the canonical Abhinav diagram — your set will have several. Base candidate: the "evolutionary optimization / Pareto front" poster.
**Composition:** x = share of 10³ futures meeting all three 2050 targets; y = acceptance margin. Failing pathways as red ghost dots, robust set in sage, the chosen pathway circled in terracotta with dashed crosshairs to both axes. A curved arrow: `more ambition = less margin`. Below, five chosen paths A–E drop into a thin strip where their passenger and freight **signposts** sit as stations on a line. A small **receipts strip** along the bottom edge.
**Narration that ADDS depth:**
- Caption: *"Robust is not optimal. It is the path that still works when the future refuses to cooperate."*
- Selected-point label: `chosen: survives the widest shift in λ`.
- Axis depth note: `y = acceptance margin — the largest rise in the threshold a path survives, reported as a result`.
- Receipts strip (the systems-thinking proof, lettered small): `choice pipeline reproduced on real Swissmetro data (LL −5214.9)` · `fast engine within ~15% of the network model` · `87 tests, traceable to commit`.
