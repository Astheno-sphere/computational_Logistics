why this is openThe evidence on acceptance disagrees, and that is the opening

Whether a charge survives depends on whether people come to accept it, and the best evidence points in two directions. In Gothenburg, support rose after the congestion charge was introduced; the authors trace it mainly to status quo bias, not to benefits exceeding expectationsborjesson16. In a randomised field trial in the Oslo region, experiencing road pricing changed attitudes only a little, while concrete information about one's own costs moved support mosttoi2179. After Oslo's permanent toll restructuring in 2019, the share positive to tolls rosetoi2141.

One reading reconciles them. A status quo can only form around a charge that is real and permanent. A trial is neither. Kristiansund's package, collecting for up to fifteen yearsinnst337, is exactly that condition, a few months old, in a town that can be surveyed.

H1. Support rises within the same people as a permanent charge becomes the status quo.

H2. Information about personal cost and where the revenue goes moves support independently of experience.

Neither has been tested in a Norwegian town living with a new permanent charge, and neither has been carried into the models that plan the path to 2050. The national models were built to compute equilibria, and TØI's own review asks how far they can describe a path toward 2050toi2051; they do not carry acceptance; the national fleet model follows vintages in aggregatebig; backcasting starts from the target and assumes that people follow.

where it is testedOne bridge, no way around

Test bed: Kristiansund

A city package with two toll stations on rv. 70 charging both ways, about 70% toll-financedinnst337. Approved by the city council 26 to 19 and the county council 33 to 14 in 2024bypakke, on an official traffic and revenue forecast, with collection from 2026. A live decision, a forecast to test, and a population that crosses the charge.

Control: Molde

The neighbouring regional centre without this package. The plain contrast between the towns is reported as description only, because Kristiansund chose its package; the causal claims rest on randomised information and on change within persons.

Reach: Norway

The question is national and so is the evidence the design argues with. The model is transferred to Molde with a stated transfer error. National implications are drawn through TØI's national evidence and the national instruments, not by pretending two towns stand for all.

howThree articles, one engine

A1 · YEAR 1 · TRANSPLAN RESEARCH AREA 3

What moves acceptance

Does support for a permanent charge rise with experience, with information, or both?

Stated choice in both towns with randomised messages on personal cost, where the revenue goes and fairness; the messages carry the causal claim. Exposure inside Kristiansund is self-reported crossing frequency, validated in aggregate by station counts; with consent, respondents may donate their own passage statement. A hybrid choice model links trust and fairness to acceptance. Submitted on wave 1; wave 2 measures the change within persons.

A2 · YEAR 2 · RESEARCH AREA 2

An engine of people and vehicles

How do revenue, emissions, who pays and support move together as the fleet turns electric?

Households carry car, trips and opinion; opinions update each year by the rule A1 estimates. Vans and lorries renew their fleets with the same turnover machinery, calibrated on the register and toll passages by class. Each year: revenue against debt service, emissions well to wheel with vehicle production, and who pays (residents, commuters from outside, firms). Validated by scoring the engine and the official forecast against observed counts, and by a 2010 to 2025 fleet hindcastwelde19,tveter25.

A3 · YEAR 3 · RESEARCH AREA 1

The order that survives

Which order of instruments stays effective, solvent and accepted across the futures nobody can forecast?

A pathway orders one to four instruments (package revision, electric rate, distance charge, earmarking), each switch set by a signpost: 904 pathways, enumerated in fulldapp. It fails politically when support drops below a threshold sampled from Norwegian toll decisions, financially when revenue stays below debt service three years running. Search maximises the futures that reach the 2050 target without failuremordm.

The field

A test of why acceptance moves, in the one setting that can separate experience from information, and the first robust search in which acceptance can end a plan.

it already runsThe engine exists; the question is new

A working prototype of the chain is public: a choice model whose estimator recovers known parameters, household agents with fleet turnover, welfare and public money per commuter, and an ensemble across futures, with automated tests on every change. It was built for an earlier question, commuter emissions, and is being retargeted to vehicle classes, toll revenue against debt and support. The bridge analysis on page 3 already runs on the same road tools.

On the prototype's speed, 0.1 s per run, the full A3 design costs about 125 CPU hours. Routing on the road network costs 100 to 1,000 times more per run, so it only checks the zonal engine on a sample, which also tests whether traffic diverts around the stations.
